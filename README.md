# 📊 Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built using the **Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dataset**. This dashboard provides business insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and monthly profits through dynamic visualizations and interactive filters.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze an e-commerce sales dataset and build an interactive dashboard that enables users to explore business performance efficiently.

Using **Power BI**, **Power Query**, **Data Modeling**, and **DAX**, raw sales data was transformed into an informative dashboard for better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing cities.
- Monitor monthly profit trends.
- Understand category-wise sales distribution.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare payment method usage.
- Identify high-profit products.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- 💰 Total Sales Amount
- 📈 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold
- 🛒 Average Order Value (AOV)

### Interactive Filters

- Quarter Filter
- City Filter

### Visualizations

- Sales Amount by City
- Quantity by Product Category
- Monthly Profit Analysis
- Top Customers by Sales
- Quantity by Payment Mode
- Profit by Product

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Indore generated the highest sales among all cities.
- Clothing contributed the largest share of total quantity sold.
- Monthly profits fluctuated significantly, with some months recording losses.
- A small group of customers generated a major portion of total sales.
- Payment methods showed uneven customer preferences.
- Certain product categories contributed significantly more profit than others.
